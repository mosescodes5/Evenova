Fees & Payouts

Simple, transparent pricing

Evenova takes a flat 5% platform fee on paid tickets — nothing else, no hidden charges. You decide who covers it.

🎟️ Attendee pays it (default)

The 5% is added on top at checkout. You receive the full ticket price you set.

Your ticket price₦10,000
+ 5% service fee₦500
Attendee pays₦10,500

💜 You absorb it

Attendees pay exactly your listed price. The 5% comes out of your payout instead.

Attendee pays₦10,000
− 5% service fee₦500
You receive₦9,500

🏷️ Discount codes Lower your fee

Evenova occasionally issues discount codes (for example, to early waitlist organizers hosting paid events) that reduce your platform fee below 5%. Redeem one anytime from Payout Account settings — it applies automatically to every future paid event, no need to re-enter it.

Getting Paid

Your wallet & withdrawals

💳 One saved payout account

📊 Live balance & history

See your available balance, every credit from ticket sales, and every withdrawal — all in one place, updated in real time.

Step-by-Step

Guides

Everything above, walked through — from your first event to your first payout.

1

Create your first event

From your Dashboard, select Create Event.

1. Choose Standard Event or Wedding — this decides the rest of the flow.
2. Fill in the title, date, time, and venue.
3. Choose Free or Paid, and set up your ticket tiers (or your guest list, for weddings).
4. Customize your registration or RSVP form.
5. Publish — your event's public page is live immediately.
2

Set up your payout account

Before your first paid event sells, add where the money should go.

1. Go to Payout Account in the sidebar.
2. Select your bank and enter your account number.
3. Confirm the account name that comes back matches you.
4. Save — every future withdrawal uses this automatically.
💡 Got a discount code? Redeem it on this same page to lower your platform fee.
3

Run check-in on event day

Get your team ready before doors open.

1. In Team, add staff accounts and assign each to a gate.
2. Staff sign in and open Scanner on their phone.
3. Scan each attendee's QR ticket — valid tickets are approved instantly.
4. Watch check-in numbers update live on your own Live Dashboard.
4

Plan a wedding

The guest experience is the whole point here — take a little extra time on this one.

1. Create Event → choose Wedding.
2. Add both your names and, if you'd like, your story — guests will see this on their invite.
3. Build your guest list — add names one at a time or paste a whole list at once, with a party size for each.
4. Add any RSVP questions — meal choice and dietary needs are one click to add.
5. After creating, open Guest List to copy each guest's personal link and send it however you like.
💡 Guests can revisit their link anytime to change their RSVP — you'll always see the latest answer.
5

Withdraw your earnings

Move money from your Evenova balance to your bank account.

1. Open Wallet to see your current balance.
2. Select Withdraw — your saved payout account fills in automatically.
3. Confirm the amount and submit.
4. Track its status right there — pending, approved, then paid.
6

Install Evenova on your phone

🍎 iOS 🤖 Android
iOS: Open Evenova in Safari → tap the Share icon → Add to Home Screen.
Android: Open Evenova in Chrome → you'll see an Install prompt — tap it. (Or: menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen.)

Either way, it opens full-screen from your home screen from then on — no browser bar, just the app.
